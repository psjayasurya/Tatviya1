Tatviya Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 | Last Revised: October 2026

Summary for Users: Tatviya respects your privacy. We collect data solely to provide digital growth services, conduct channel due diligence, and execute secure transaction escrow. We never sell your personal data. You retain full rights to inspect, correct, or erase your information at any time.

1. Scope & Legal Framework

This Privacy Policy applies to all services, websites, tools, and escrow workflows provided by Tatviya ("we", "us", "our"), having its registered operations at 16-6-457 1st Floor Karnala Layout, Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh – 517501, India.

We process personal data in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) of India, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and where applicable, international standards such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit our consultation form, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number.
  • Inquiry & Channel Data: Selected service category, inquiry subject, detailed message, YouTube channel URL, subscriber metrics, and public analytics submitted for valuation or trading.
  • Due Diligence & KYC Data (Transaction Counterparties): Government-issued identity documentation (e.g., PAN card, Aadhaar, Passport) and bank payout details required solely for executing legally binding escrow agreements and anti-money laundering (AML) compliance.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, and timestamp telemetry collected strictly for DDoS mitigation, anti-spam validation, and security rate limiting.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

We collect and process your personal data strictly for specified, legitimate commercial purposes:

  1. Service Delivery: Evaluating YouTube channel eligibility, accelerating watch hours, and executing digital marketing campaigns.
  2. Escrow Brokerage & Due Diligence: Verifying channel copyright history, authenticating seller ownership, drafting Bill of Sale contracts, and coordinating 7-day Google Brand Account transfers.
  3. Client Communication: Responding to contact inquiries, scheduling consultations, and delivering transaction milestone status reports.
  4. Fraud Prevention & Security: Defending against bot spam, automated crawlers, and fraudulent channel recovery disputes.

4. Data Retention Schedule

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered:

  • General Inquiries: Communications that do not lead to an active contract are securely purged from our systems within 180 days.
  • Active Transaction Records: Signed escrow contracts, Bill of Sale documentation, and payment proofs are retained for 5 years in compliance with Indian statutory commercial and accounting regulations.
  • Due Diligence Screenshots: Private analytics provided for audit purposes are permanently deleted within 30 days following transaction completion.

5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

All YouTube channel analytics, revenue screenshots, and valuation metrics submitted to Tatviya are treated as strictly confidential. Our team members operate under binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). We do not sell, rent, or lease client information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

6. Security Measures

We maintain technical, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, or alteration. These include:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / 256-bit SSL) across all website and API communications.
  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP) and Subresource Integrity (SRI) on external assets.
  • Server-side input sanitization, anti-header injection protocols, and IP rate limiting.

7. Your Legal Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable privacy laws, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of personal data held by Tatviya.
  • Right to Correction: Request prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of personal data no longer necessary for the legal purpose collected.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit complaints directly to our designated Grievance Officer.
