3. YouTube Channel Trading & Escrow Rules

Any purchase, sale, or transfer of a YouTube channel brokered through Tatviya is strictly governed by the following covenants:

  1. Mandatory Escrow Holding: Prior to the initiation of any ownership invitations or account credential sharing, the buyer must deposit 100% of the agreed transaction consideration into Tatviya's designated escrow holding account.
  2. Google Brand Account Protocol: All channel handovers must be executed exclusively via the Google Brand Account delegation architecture. Direct transfers of personal Google accounts or shared passwords without Brand Account migration are prohibited.
  3. Mandatory 7-Day Waiting Period: Under Google LLC's security policy, when a new Owner is added to a Brand Account, a mandatory 7-day waiting period must elapse before that user can be designated as the "Primary Owner". Escrow funds will remain locked in Tatviya's escrow during this entire 7-day period.
  4. Escrow Disbursement Condition: Tatviya releases funds to the seller only after the buyer confirms: (a) designation as sole Primary Owner; (b) removal of prior owners; and (c) successful update of 2-factor authentication and recovery options.

4. Seller Representations & Warranties

Each seller representing a channel on Tatviya explicitly warrants and covenants that:

  • They hold legitimate, unencumbered ownership of the YouTube channel and associated Brand Account.
  • The channel has zero active Community Guideline strikes, copyright strikes, or pending monetization sanctions.
  • The channel's watch hours, subscribers, and views have been generated through organic audience engagement, and not through bot networks, automated view generators, or fraudulent click farms.
  • There are no outstanding third-party claims, commercial liens, or co-creator disputes regarding the channel's revenues.

5. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers engaging in channel transactions agree to:

  • Complete their due diligence and inspect analytics read-access within 48 hours of invitation.
  • Maintain active communication during the 7-day primary ownership migration window.
  • Immediately secure the Brand Account upon receipt of Primary Ownership by configuring independent two-factor authentication (2FA) and unique recovery security credentials.
