Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 | Last Revised: October 2026

100% Escrow Buyer Protection: If a channel fails due diligence verification, or the seller fails to deliver uncompromised Primary Ownership within the agreed transfer window, 100% of the buyer's deposited escrow funds are promptly refunded.

1. Overview

At Tatviya, we operate with complete financial transparency. This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds, cancellations, and escrow disbursements are processed across our service categories.

2. YouTube Channel Trading Escrow Protection

Channel trading transactions are governed by our milestone-based escrow mechanism:

  • 100% Refundable Triggers for Buyers: A buyer is entitled to an immediate 100% refund of deposited escrow funds if:
    • The channel fails forensic analytics due diligence (e.g., active strikes discovered, undisclosed copyright issues, or non-genuine bot traffic).
    • The seller fails to issue the Google Brand Account invitation within the agreed 48-hour timeframe.
    • The seller revokes permissions or fails to transfer Primary Ownership after the 7-day Google waiting period has concluded.
  • Non-Refundable Condition: Once the buyer confirms that Primary Ownership of the YouTube Brand Account has been successfully received, all prior owners removed, and security credentials updated, the transaction is marked completed. At this stage, funds are released to the seller and the transaction is final and non-refundable.

3. YouTube Monetization & Consulting Services

For watch hour acceleration, metadata optimization, and audience growth consulting:

  • Cancellation Before Work Commencement: If a client requests cancellation within 24 hours of booking and prior to the commencement of algorithmic audit work, a 100% refund will be issued.
  • Partial Refunds: Once audit and strategic implementation has begun, fees are refundable on a prorated basis according to completed milestones.

4. Digital Marketing & Paid Advertising Campaigns

For Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) and Google/YouTube Ads management:

  • Direct ad spend paid to ad platforms (Meta, Google) is non-refundable by Tatviya, as it is consumed directly by third-party advertising networks.
  • Agency management retainers can be cancelled with 14 days written notice prior to the start of the next billing cycle.

5. Website Design & Development

For custom web engineering and business website development projects:

  • Initial discovery deposits are refundable if cancelled before UI/UX design wireframes are delivered.
  • Subsequent milestone payments correspond to verified deliverables (Wireframes $\rightarrow$ Functional Code $\rightarrow$ Production Deployment) and are non-refundable once signed off by the client.

6. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed through the original method of payment:

  • Escrow Return: Returned via verified NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, or bank transfer within 2 to 3 business days of approval.
  • Online Payment Gateway: Processed within 5 to 7 business days, subject to the cardholder's issuing bank policies.
