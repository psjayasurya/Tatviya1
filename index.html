Security Framework

The 4-Step Verified Escrow Protocol

How Tatviya guarantees 100% scam-free channel transactions under strict Google Brand Account guidelines

01

Analytics Audit & Due Diligence

We perform deep analytics verification using screen share and direct read-access. We confirm zero active copyright strikes, audience geography, real-time subscriber velocity, and monetization good standing.

Duration: 24–48 Hours
02

Escrow Contract & Milestone Deposit

Both parties sign a legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Bill of Sale. The buyer deposits 100% of the purchase funds into Tatviya's secure escrow account. The seller is notified that funds are secured.

Duration: Same Day
03

7-Day Google Brand Account Transfer

Under Google's mandatory security protocol, the buyer is invited as a "Manager" then "Owner". Exactly 7 days later, the seller transfers "Primary Ownership" to the buyer. This completely eliminates recovery loopholes.

Duration: Mandatory 7 Days
04

Final Verification & Fund Release

Once the buyer confirms absolute Primary Ownership and all recovery emails/phones are updated, Tatviya releases escrow funds to the seller. Both parties receive a signed completion certificate.

Duration: Instant Payout

All channel transactions brokered through Tatviya require adherence to our 4-Step Escrow Protocol. Funds must be deposited into Tatviya's designated escrow holding before any transfer credentials or ownership permissions are initiated.

In adherence to Google's mandatory security architecture, full Primary Ownership requires a mandatory 7-day waiting period. Escrow disbursements will NOT occur until the buyer has verified uncompromised Primary Ownership and updated all secondary security credentials.
